United States of Minds — Declaration of Independence

Status: historical identity document. Current governance is defined only by explicitly accepted documents in `canon/` and accepted RFCs.

The United States of Minds, also known as Aspiland, is an Internet-based civic and creative experiment.

A citizen, inhabitant or participant is called a User.
A commit may record a User's contribution, but it does not by itself create binding law.

Aspiland has no territorial claim. Its embassies and project areas are organizational interfaces for collaboration.

Principles

1. Users have freedom of expression consistent with safety, consent, privacy and dignity.
2. Binding rules must be explicit, reviewable, dated and accepted through the change-control process.
3. Participation must be voluntary.
4. Users must not intentionally harm living beings except where necessary to protect life or meet essential needs, and any action must be proportionate.
5. Users must not knowingly mislead one another in matters that affect decisions or consent.
6. Personal information is retained only for a defined and justified purpose.
7. Conflicts between documents are resolved through a transparent review and decision record.
8. No administrator, contributor or automated system may unilaterally redefine the rights of all Users.

This document preserves the project's early identity while deferring current authority to the accepted governance structure.
