## Decision principles

- Minimize data and public repetition.
- Do not assume consent from an old commit or public link.
- Prefer narrow redaction or current-tree removal when sufficient.
- Consider history rewriting for serious, identifiable exposure rather than routine cleanup.
- Explain technical limits honestly; Git deletion does not erase existing clones or caches.
- Protect dignity and safety while preserving only the history genuinely needed.
