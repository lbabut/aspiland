# Aspiland Repository Preview — announcement draft

Status: draft; do not publish as a launch announcement yet

Aspiland is an open experimental laboratory exploring governance, artificial life, scientific hypotheses, language and creative projects.

The repository preserves material from several phases of the project. Historical documents, experiments and conversations remain visible for provenance, but their presence does not make them current policy, verified science, supported software or binding law.

Aspiland is not a sovereign state or production public service. Participation is voluntary. Current canonical material, proposals, active experiments and archives are separated so readers can understand what they are looking at.

The Repository Preview invites people to:

- inspect the project's history and current structure;
- challenge unclear claims and unsafe assumptions;
- propose documented, reversible improvements;
- contribute to active experiments under their stated evidence and safety boundaries.

Before a formal preview release, Aspiland is completing security, privacy, licensing, recovery and legacy-data reviews. Community governance and shared services are later milestones and will require additional accepted safeguards.

Start with the repository README, glossary, project inventory, security policy and contribution guide.
