# Aspiland glossary

Status: explanatory; non-canonical  
Last reviewed: 2026-07-12

This glossary defines how terms are used in the current repository. It does not create rights or authority. If it conflicts with an accepted canonical document, the canonical document controls and the glossary should be updated.

## Preferred current terms

### Aspiland

The name of this open repository and its governance, software, research and creative experiments. Aspiland is not a sovereign state, legal jurisdiction or production public service.

### Participant

A person who voluntarily reads, discusses, contributes to or uses part of the project. This is the preferred general term when no technical role is intended.

### User

A person or technical identity interacting with a particular system, repository or experiment. `User` does not automatically mean participant, member or citizen.

### Repository owner

The GitHub account with platform-level control over the repository. Repository ownership is an operational fact, not unlimited governance authority.

### Maintainer

A person entrusted with a documented area of work, such as reviewing changes, operating infrastructure or maintaining a project. The scope, access and succession path should be explicit.

### Administrator

A role with elevated technical permissions. Administrative access does not create authority outside its documented scope and should follow least privilege, review and revocation procedures.

### Automated system or agent

Software that proposes, checks, summarizes or performs defined operations. An automated system may assist governance but cannot independently create canonical rules, determine rights or impose irreversible consequences.

### Canon

The set of documents explicitly accepted as currently binding within Aspiland through the required review, decision, versioning, effective-date and rollback process.

### Canonical

Having accepted status within `canon/`. A document is not canonical merely because it exists, is old, is on the default branch or uses constitutional language.

### RFC

A numbered proposal or decision record stored under `rfc/`. A merged RFC can remain `Proposed`; acceptance requires an explicit decision and effective date.

### Decision record

A durable record of what was decided, by which process, on what date, with what scope and with which review or appeal path.

### Effective date

The date on which an accepted rule or decision starts applying. Repository merge time and effective date may differ.

### Activation window

The period between notice or acceptance and the effective date, allowing affected participants to review, prepare, object or request clarification. Historical material may call this `Vacatio Legis`.

### Protocol

A documented procedure for recurring operations such as change control, consent, review, succession, incident response or rollback.

### Project

A bounded experiment, research effort or software system under `projects/` or a clearly identified legacy area. A project's internal rules are not automatically repository-wide governance.

### Archive

Preserved historical or superseded material. Archiving records provenance; it does not endorse the content or make it current.

### Public material

Content intentionally reviewed for unrestricted publication. Presence elsewhere in a public repository does not prove that the content received a privacy or publication review.

### Legacy material

Content retained from an earlier phase whose current status, accuracy, safety or ownership may be unresolved. Legacy material is non-canonical unless separately accepted.

### Review

A meaningful opportunity for affected people to inspect, question and challenge a proposal before or after a decision, according to its risk and scope.

### Affected participant

A person whose access, data, rights, responsibilities, safety or reasonable expectations may materially change because of a decision.

### Consent

A specific, informed and voluntary agreement that can be withheld without improper pressure. Consent should be scoped, recorded when consequential and revocable where practical.

### Rollback

A defined method to reverse, disable or supersede a change while preserving an understandable decision history.

### Emergency measure

A narrow, temporary and documented action taken to reduce immediate harm. It must have an expiry condition and later review; emergency status does not create permanent authority.

### Personal data

Information relating to an identified or reasonably identifiable person. It should be collected and retained only for a defined, justified purpose with appropriate access and deletion controls.

### Secret

A credential or value that grants access or proves authority, including private keys, passwords, tokens, recovery codes and signing material. A disclosed secret must be assumed compromised and rotated or revoked.

### Essential shared infrastructure

A resource whose loss would materially prevent participation, recovery, communication or access to important project data. Restrictions affecting it require heightened review.

### Sensory boundary

A voluntarily communicated limit or accommodation related to noise, light, smell, proximity, interruption or communication cadence. It should not require disclosure of a diagnosis.

### Go-live

An intentional transition from internal preparation to a publicly announced and supported mode of operation. Aspiland distinguishes repository launch from community or governance launch.

### Repository launch

Publicly presenting the repository as an experimental, inspectable body of work and inviting issues or contributions. It does not establish membership, citizenship, jurisdiction or binding community governance.

### Community or governance launch

Inviting people to participate under shared rules, roles, decisions or services. This requires accepted governance, onboarding, privacy, security, accessibility, moderation, appeal and succession procedures beyond those needed for a repository launch.

## Historical or ambiguous terms

| Historical term | Current interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| `Aspieland`, `AspieLand` | Historical spellings of `Aspiland`; preserve in source paths and quotations but prefer `Aspiland` in new material. |
| `State`, `Państwo` | Historical metaphor for the project; not a claim of sovereignty or legal jurisdiction. |
| `Citizen`, `Obywatel` | Historical participant label; not legal nationality, residency or citizenship. Prefer `participant` unless an accepted experiment defines a narrower meaning. |
| `Constitution`, `Konstytucja` | A constitutional draft or experiment unless explicitly accepted into `canon/`. |
| `Global Admin` and similar admin groups | Historical technical or governance role proposals. They have no current authority unless redefined and accepted through review. |
| `Prod` | A historical workflow state. The applicable version is the one explicitly accepted with an effective date, not merely a branch or environment called production. |
| `Vacatio Legis` | Prefer `activation window` or `review window` for project procedures. |
| `Forbidden Emission` | Historical term for excessive sensory impact. Prefer `sensory impact`, `sensory boundary` or a precise description of the affected people and conditions. |
| `Hajs` | Historical experimental token or accounting unit. It has no guaranteed financial value, entitlement or connection to essential access. |
| `Embassy`, `Ambasada` | Historical organizational metaphor; not diplomatic status or immunity. |
| `Territory`, `Terytorium` | Historical metaphor or opt-in project space; not a legal land claim or override of local law. |
| `Ownership`, `Własność` | May mean repository ownership, intellectual-property rights, physical property or stewardship. New documents must state which meaning applies. |

## Usage rule

When introducing a consequential new term, define it in the proposal and add it here after review. Avoid silently assigning legal, medical or financial meanings to project metaphors.
