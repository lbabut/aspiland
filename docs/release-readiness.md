# Release readiness summary

Status: repository launch complete; non-canonical  
Last reviewed: 2026-07-16

## Current milestone

**Formal public repository launch — GO.**

Aspiland is publicly presented as an experimental repository and historical archive. The launch does not claim production support, sovereign status, legal jurisdiction or a fully operating governed community.

- current root overview, repository map and glossary;
- separation of canon, RFCs, protocols, projects, archive and public material;
- contribution, conduct, review and change-control guidance;
- security-reporting and exposed-secret procedures;
- current-tree and Git-history secret review;
- current-tree privacy and unsafe-material remediation;
- invalidation of the known historical credential candidate;
- connected-Drive credential review and bounded external-store risk acceptance;
- protected `main`, automated quality checks and secret guards;
- project inventory and unfamiliar-reader onboarding review;
- successful recovery rehearsal and verified encrypted offsite backup;
- formal launch decision, public note, release name and tag.

## Accepted residual risks

- external stores were not exhaustively searched;
- historical material may be unsupported or of uncertain provenance;
- the repository has no project-wide license;
- repository review does not constitute production, legal or forensic certification.

The decision and boundaries are recorded in `docs/decisions/2026-07-16-repository-launch-go.md`.

## Ongoing repository work

- classify and document remaining legacy areas;
- refresh the offsite backup after consequential changes;
- keep quality and secret checks active;
- maintain clear public status and rollback guidance;
- review the launch decision by 2026-08-16.

## Community launch blockers

A community or governance launch additionally requires:

- explicit acceptance of a minimal canonical package;
- roles, succession, notice, appeal and moderation procedures;
- an operational data lifecycle and participant request path;
- accessibility and sensory-boundary handling;
- service ownership and incident response;
- separation of experimental economy from identity and essential access.

## Current recommendation

Maintain the formally launched repository under the recorded controls and boundaries. Do not describe Aspiland as an operating governed community until the separate community-launch gates and decision process are complete.
