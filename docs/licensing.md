# Licensing status

Status: unresolved; no repository-wide reuse license granted

Aspiland is publicly visible, but public visibility is not the same as an open-source or open-content license.

## Current rule

Until an explicit rights review and owner decision are completed:

- no root license should be assumed to cover the repository;
- historical contributions, conversations and user areas have unclear reuse rights unless documented otherwise;
- third-party material remains subject to its original terms;
- artwork, names, logos and merchandise designs are not automatically licensed with software;
- contributors should submit only material they have authority to contribute.

RFC 0007 proposes a mixed-content licensing strategy and lists decisions still required. It does not itself grant permissions.

## Planned structure

After approval and rights verification, Aspiland may use path-specific licensing for:

- new original software;
- new documentation and governance text;
- research data and generated outputs;
- artwork and branding;
- reviewed historical material;
- third-party notices and attributions.

## Contribution warning

Do not copy code, images, articles, datasets or other material into Aspiland merely because they are accessible online. Link to the source when redistribution rights are unclear, and record attribution and license when reuse is authorized.
