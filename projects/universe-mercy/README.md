# UNIVERSE / MERCY

Status: Conceptual project / ethical thought experiment

## Mission

Explore how humanity should govern hypothetical technologies capable of making highly consequential interventions in biological, environmental, informational or other complex systems.

The project does not claim that a reality-editing technology currently exists.

## Core safeguards

- informed consent,
- necessity and proportionality,
- minimal intervention,
- reversibility and preserved prior states,
- independent oversight and distributed control,
- transparency and auditability,
- protection of identity, autonomy and dignity,
- equitable access,
- preservation of multiple viable futures.

## Foundational question

What exactly are we trying to save?

## Relationship to Aspiland

UNIVERSE / MERCY is the first project organized under Aspiland's proposed non-destructive governance structure. It is not part of the canon unless and until specific documents are accepted through the change-control process.
