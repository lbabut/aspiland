# RFC 0003 — Remove obsolete coercive and persistence rules

Status: Accepted  
Accepted: 2026-07-12  
Decision record: repository owner instruction  
Effective: 2026-07-12

## Purpose

Remove obsolete legacy rules that conflict with consent, dignity, privacy, proportionality, reversible governance and fair process.

## Removed categories

- irreversible bodily sanctions;
- automatic loss of citizenship or property solely because of debt;
- unlimited claims about speech or behaviour that ignore harm, consent or privacy;
- precedence based only on the age of a document;
- indefinite data retention without a defined purpose;
- technical copy-count rules presented as constitutional authority;
- administrator self-expansion without review;
- duplicate temporary drafts containing superseded rules.

## Replacement principles

- sanctions must be proportionate, time-limited, reviewable and as reversible as practical;
- property changes require consent or a fair, transparent process with appeal;
- expression is protected together with safety, privacy, consent and dignity;
- the applicable version is the one explicitly accepted and given an effective date;
- personal information is retained only for a defined and justified purpose;
- operational storage practices do not determine constitutional authority;
- no person or automated system may unilaterally redefine everyone’s rights.
