# Aspiland public brand system

Status: public communication baseline; non-canonical  
Last reviewed: 2026-07-12

This guide keeps Aspiland recognizable without presenting an experiment as a sovereign state, legal jurisdiction, production service or completed community.

## Positioning

Aspiland is an open repository and long-running laboratory for governance, software, artificial life, research and creative experiments.

The public framing for the current phase is:

> **Repository Preview — an open prototype of a digital civilization.**

The phrase describes an inspectable body of work. It does not declare a community or governance go-live.

## Primary line

> **A world you do not join through a form. You enter through a commit.**

Polish:

> **Świat, do którego nie zapisujesz się formularzem. Wchodzisz przez commit.**

## Supporting lines

Use selectively; do not present all of them together.

- What if a civilization had version history?
- The repository is open. The future is still a draft.
- Preserve the dream. Separate the layers.
- Memory is not authority.
- Make the world clearer than you found it.
- A country you can fork is a historical metaphor, not a sovereignty claim.

Polish:

- Co, gdyby cywilizacja miała historię wersji?
- Repozytorium jest otwarte. Przyszłość nadal jest szkicem.
- Zachowaj marzenie. Oddziel warstwy.
- Pamięć nie jest władzą.
- Zostaw świat bardziej zrozumiały, niż go zastałeś.

## Voice

Aspiland should sound:

- imaginative, but precise about what exists;
- strange, but understandable;
- ambitious, but not grandiose about current capabilities;
- inviting, but never coercive;
- playful, but not careless with privacy, safety or authority;
- technical, but readable by a curious non-developer.

Prefer concrete verbs: read, inspect, propose, test, document, review, improve, reverse.

Avoid claims such as:

- first real digital country;
- sovereign state;
- official citizenship or nationality;
- guaranteed democratic or safe system;
- autonomous government;
- production-ready platform;
- currency, investment or financial return when discussing APS or Hajs;
- artificial intelligence with legal or supreme authority.

## Naming

Use `Aspiland` in new public material.

Preserve `AspieLand`, `Aspieland` and other historical spellings only when referring to paths, quotations or earlier project phases.

Use `participant` or `contributor` as the default current term. `Citizen` is a historical or experiment-specific metaphor and must not imply legal citizenship.

## Visual direction

The current website implements the visual system without third-party assets.

### Concept: Orbit Gate

The mark combines:

- a circle representing a bounded, inspectable world;
- three lines forming an abstract `A` and an open gate;
- orbital paths representing canon, proposals and projects;
- no crown, military symbol, territorial outline or official-state seal.

### Palette

- Void: `#080B12`
- Archive: `#0F1420`
- Signal: `#9CF6D8`
- Signal strong: `#41DCA8`
- Primary text: `#F5F7FB`
- Secondary text: `#9DA9BD`
- Review warning: `#FFCF8A`

The warning color indicates unresolved status or review, not danger by default.

### Typography

Use a clean system sans-serif for general text and a system monospace for paths, statuses and repository signals. The public website must not depend on externally hosted fonts.

### Layout

- large, direct statements;
- visible whitespace;
- repository paths as navigational objects;
- thin boundaries rather than heavy boxes;
- status and disclaimers placed near claims, not hidden in footnotes;
- no decorative element should block keyboard navigation or readable contrast.

## Required public disclaimer

Use this near a prominent introduction:

> Aspiland is an experiment, not a sovereign state, legal jurisdiction or production public service.

Polish:

> Aspiland jest eksperymentem, a nie suwerennym państwem, jurysdykcją prawną ani produkcyjną usługą publiczną.

## Status vocabulary

Use these exact meanings:

- **Repository Preview** — public presentation of inspectable work; contributions may be proposed.
- **Repository launch** — an intentional public release after repository-level launch gates are complete.
- **Community launch** — participation under shared operational rules and services; currently not ready.
- **Proposed** — recorded for review, not accepted.
- **Accepted** — explicitly approved through the applicable process.
- **Canonical** — accepted into `canon/` with effective status.
- **Archived** — preserved as history, not current authority.

## Asset rules

Before publishing a visual or document:

1. confirm that it contains no personal data, credentials, private infrastructure or unlicensed third-party material;
2. label fiction, satire, historical material and proposals clearly;
3. keep the experimental disclaimer visible when the context could imply a real state or service;
4. retain editable source files in the repository;
5. document the source version for translations;
6. do not use official national emblems, seals or documents in a way that creates confusion.

## Initial reusable formats

The first release should support:

- website hero and repository preview page;
- square social card;
- horizontal announcement card;
- one-page project introduction;
- press and partner summary;
- First Commit Kit;
- project and RFC announcement cards;
- historical-artifact posts clearly labelled as archive or fiction.

Graphics derived from this system remain public communication material and do not become canonical governance by publication.
