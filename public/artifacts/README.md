# Public world artifacts

Status: public creative templates; non-canonical  
Last reviewed: 2026-07-12

These templates turn Aspiland concepts into recognizable public artifacts without creating legal identity, diplomatic status, financial value, property rights or governance authority.

Every published artifact must be clearly marked as an experimental or commemorative project document.

## Common footer

Use this footer on every artifact:

> **ASPILAND PROJECT ARTIFACT — EXPERIMENTAL / NON-OFFICIAL**  
> This document grants no legal identity, nationality, residency, diplomatic status, ownership, financial value, access right or governance authority. Verify current project status in the public repository.

Polish:

> **ARTEFAKT PROJEKTU ASPILAND — EKSPERYMENTALNY / NIEOFICJALNY**  
> Dokument nie nadaje tożsamości prawnej, obywatelstwa, rezydencji, statusu dyplomatycznego, własności, wartości finansowej, prawa dostępu ani władzy zarządczej. Aktualny status projektu należy sprawdzić w publicznym repozytorium.

## 1. First Commit record

Preferred name:

**First Commit Record**

Avoid `citizenship certificate`, `naturalization`, `state certificate` or claims of official recognition.

### Front

**ASPILAND**  
**FIRST COMMIT RECORDED**

This record recognizes a reviewed contribution that made the repository clearer, safer or more useful.

- Contribution:
- Repository path or pull request:
- Date merged or accepted:
- Contribution type:
- Source commit:

### Meaning

The contribution history is the source of truth. The record grants no legal identity, rank, ownership, financial value or governance authority.

### Verification

Include a human-readable repository link and optional QR code only after checking that it points to a permanent public record and contains no tracking parameters.

## 2. Participant field card

Preferred name:

**Participant Field Card**

This is a creative orientation card, not identification.

### Fields

- Display name or pseudonym — optional;
- contribution areas — voluntary;
- public repository profile — optional;
- languages — optional;
- accessibility or communication preferences — optional and non-diagnostic;
- card edition and source commit;
- expiry or review date.

### Required front label

`PROJECT ORIENTATION CARD — NOT IDENTITY DOCUMENT`

Do not include date of birth, legal address, government identifiers, medical diagnoses, signatures copied from official documents or biometric data.

## 3. Repository passport

Preferred public label:

**Repository Passport — Creative Edition**

The object may visually reference travel logs but must not resemble a specific country's passport closely enough to cause confusion.

### Cover

**ASPILAND**  
**REPOSITORY PASSPORT**  
`CREATIVE EDITION / NOT A TRAVEL DOCUMENT`

### Suggested pages

- map of repository layers;
- First Commit record;
- project visits;
- RFCs read or reviewed;
- archive discoveries;
- translations contributed;
- accessibility or safety improvements;
- blank pages for future contribution stamps.

### Stamp language

Use:

- `REPOSITORY VISITED`;
- `CONTRIBUTION REVIEWED`;
- `PROJECT DOCUMENTED`;
- `RFC COMMENTED`;
- `ARCHIVE CLASSIFIED`;
- `TRANSLATION CHECKED`.

Do not use immigration, border-control, visa, customs, citizenship or diplomatic language in a way that could imply official status.

## 4. Embassy plaque

Preferred label:

**Aspiland Project Node**

Historical `Embassy` wording may appear as a subtitle when its metaphorical status is explicit.

### Text

**ASPILAND PROJECT NODE**  
A voluntary place for learning, contribution and connection to the public repository.

`HISTORICAL NAME: EMBASSY / NO DIPLOMATIC STATUS`

The plaque must not claim immunity, territorial jurisdiction, official representation or exemption from local law.

## 5. APS specimen note

Preferred label:

**APS Experiment Specimen**

### Front

**ASPILAND PROJECT**  
**APS EXPERIMENT SPECIMEN**  
`NO MONETARY VALUE`

### Back

APS is a historical experimental token or accounting concept. This specimen is a creative artifact. It is not legal tender, a security, investment, benefit, wage, debt, entitlement or promise of exchange.

Do not include exchange rates, investment-return claims, guaranteed scarcity, payment instructions, wallet addresses or purchase calls until a separately reviewed and lawful experiment explicitly requires them.

## 6. Decision seal

Preferred label:

**Reviewed Decision Mark**

The mark may be applied only when a source record identifies:

- proposal or decision number;
- status;
- decision date;
- effective date when applicable;
- decision authority or process;
- review or rollback path;
- source commit.

The mark must never turn `Proposed` into `Accepted` through design alone.

Suggested status variants:

- `PROPOSED — OPEN FOR REVIEW`;
- `ACCEPTED — EFFECTIVE DATE RECORDED`;
- `SUPERSEDED — HISTORY PRESERVED`;
- `ARCHIVED — NON-CURRENT`;
- `EXPERIMENTAL — PROJECT-SCOPED`.

## 7. System Guardian mark

Preferred label:

**System Guardian — Bounded Operational Role**

Suggested ring text:

`OBSERVE / PROPOSE / MAINTAIN / NARROW CONTAIN`

Required exclusion text:

`NO OWNERSHIP / NO SOVEREIGNTY / NO UNLIMITED ADMINISTRATION`

Do not depict a crown, throne, military rank or all-seeing authority. The mark should communicate watchfulness, continuity and limits.

## 8. Archive evidence label

Use this on historical material prepared for public explanation:

**ARCHIVE EVIDENCE**  
Preserved to document project history. Not current authority. Accuracy, ownership or safety may require further review.

Include:

- original path;
- approximate phase or date when known;
- current classification;
- review date;
- link to the explanatory record rather than unnecessary personal data.

## Production checklist

- [ ] The artifact is labelled experimental, creative or commemorative.
- [ ] It does not resemble a real government document closely enough to mislead.
- [ ] It grants no identity, status, authority, ownership, money or essential access.
- [ ] It contains no secret, unnecessary personal data or biometric information.
- [ ] Any repository link points to reviewed public material.
- [ ] Historical and current terms are distinguished.
- [ ] The source commit and edition are recorded.
- [ ] Editable sources and accessible text alternatives are retained.
- [ ] Third-party fonts, symbols, artwork and templates have confirmed usage rights.
- [ ] A correction or withdrawal path exists.
